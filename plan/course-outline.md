# Course Title: Prompt Engineering for Generative AI (1 hour)

## Course Description

Generative AI models can produce impressive content, but vague or poorly structured inputs often yield inaccurate, generic, or hallucinated responses. Without structured prompting techniques, users struggle to obtain consistent, high-quality results from modern AI assistants.

This course teaches foundational prompt engineering principles to bridge the gap between intention and model output. Students learn how to establish context, specify roles and constraints, apply core prompting patterns, and iteratively refine prompts using Google Gemini.

## Learning Objectives

- Construct effective prompts to produce reliable, high-quality results from generative AI models
- Apply structured prompting techniques and iterative refinement to generate accurate outputs in Gemini

## Who Should Attend

Beginners, knowledge workers, and professionals looking to improve their productivity and output quality when working with generative AI tools.

## Prerequisites

- Basic exposure to conversational generative AI tools

## Hands-On Activities

- Lab: Engineering Effective Prompts in Gemini
